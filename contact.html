About Cathy

With over 18 years of experience, Cathy is a passionate, qualified therapist dedicated to high standards. She provides one-to-one, bespoke treatments in a beautiful purpose-built therapy room.

Member - Angelic Reiki Association ACHO Registered Practitioner BCMA Registered NCP Accredited Guild of Holistic Therapists Guild of Beauty Therapists INLPTA NLP - 18+ years Certified Munay-Ki Mentor Qualified in Anatomy and Physiology Certificate in Training Practice (CITP) DBS Checked

Cathy has delivered meditation in care homes, GP practices, sheltered housing, church recreation areas, and for Cancer Support Yorkshire. She has been providing card readings since the 1970s.

Cathy is also a member of the National Council of Psychotherapists (NCP) and delivers training with over 18 years experience in corporate environments and at Crystal Serenity Academy.
