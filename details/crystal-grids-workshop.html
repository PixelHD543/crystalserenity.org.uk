Crystal Grids Workshop

Learn to create grids for manifesting, protection, and healing

What is a Crystal Grid?

A crystal grid is a deliberate arrangement of crystals and stones, laid out in a specific geometric pattern, to focus energy toward a particular intention. Grids amplify the energy of individual crystals, creating a powerful field of intention that continues to work long after the grid is created.

In this hands-on workshop, you will learn everything you need to create your own crystal grids for manifesting, healing, protection, and more.

What You Will Learn

  • The history and purpose of crystal grids
  • How to choose the right crystals for your intention
  • Sacred geometry and grid patterns (circle, flower of life, Metatron's cube, etc.)
  • How to cleanse and program your crystals
  • Step-by-step grid creation
  • How to activate and maintain your grid
  • Grids for different purposes: manifestation, protection, healing, abundance, love, and spiritual connection

Workshop Format

This is a practical, hands-on workshop. You will create at least one crystal grid during the session to take home with you. All crystals and materials are provided, though you are welcome to bring your own crystals if you wish.

The workshop lasts approximately 3 hours, including a break for tea and reflection.

Who Is This Workshop For?

This workshop is suitable for all levels, from complete beginners to experienced crystal users who want to deepen their practice. No prior knowledge of crystals or sacred geometry is required.

Benefits of Creating Crystal Grids

  • Amplifies the power of your intentions
  • Creates a focused energy field for manifesting
  • Provides ongoing support for healing or protection
  • Deepens your connection with crystals
  • Offers a creative and meditative practice
  • Beautiful addition to your home or sacred space
