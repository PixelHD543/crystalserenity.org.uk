Magical Thinking & Exploring Sirius

Explore the power of intention and manifestation while connecting with the spiritual energies of Sirius

About This Workshop

This unique workshop combines two powerful spiritual practices: Magical Thinking (the art of conscious manifestation) and Exploring Sirius (connecting with the high-frequency energies of the star system Sirius).

Sirius has long been regarded as a spiritual centre for advanced beings, healers, and lightworkers. Connecting with Sirian energy can accelerate your spiritual growth, enhance your intuition, and support your manifestation practice.

What You Will Learn

Part 1: Magical Thinking

  • What is magical thinking and how it differs from wishful thinking
  • The principles of conscious manifestation
  • How to align your thoughts, emotions, and actions with your intentions
  • Practical techniques for daily manifestation
  • Overcoming limiting beliefs and blocks

Part 2: Exploring Sirius

  • The spiritual significance of the Sirius star system
  • How to connect with Sirian energies and beings
  • Sirian healing frequencies and how to work with them
  • Meditations to open a channel to Sirius
  • How Sirius can support your manifestation practice

Workshop Format

This workshop is delivered over one full day (10am - 5pm) and includes:

  • Talks and teachings on both topics
  • Guided meditations to connect with Sirian energy
  • Practical manifestation exercises
  • Group discussion and sharing
  • Tea, coffee, and light refreshments

Who Is This Workshop For?

This workshop is suitable for anyone interested in manifestation, spiritual development, or star wisdom. No prior experience is necessary.

Benefits of Attending

  • Learn practical tools for conscious manifestation
  • Connect with the high-frequency energies of Sirius
  • Accelerate your spiritual growth and ascension
  • Gain clarity on your intentions and life purpose
  • Meet like-minded spiritual seekers
  • Leave feeling inspired, empowered, and connected
