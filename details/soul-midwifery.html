Soul Midwifery Training

Support souls through the transition of death and rebirth

What is Soul Midwifery?

Soul Midwifery is a sacred and profound calling. A Soul Midwife supports individuals and their families through the dying process, providing emotional, spiritual, and energetic support during one of life's most significant transitions — death and rebirth.

Just as a birth midwife supports new life entering the world, a Soul Midwife supports the soul's journey as it leaves the physical body and returns to spirit.

What You Will Learn

  • The stages of dying from a spiritual perspective
  • How to create a sacred, peaceful environment for the dying
  • Energy techniques to comfort and support the departing soul
  • How to support family members and loved ones
  • Working with angels and spiritual guides during the transition
  • Soul retrieval and after-death support for the soul's journey
  • Self-care and boundaries for Soul Midwives
  • Ethics and practical considerations

Course Format

This is a deep and transformative training, usually delivered over 2-3 days (or spread across several weekends). The course includes theory, group discussion, guided meditations, and practical exercises.

Previous experience with energy healing (such as Angelic Reiki or Crystal Healing) is beneficial but not essential.

Who Is This Training For?

Soul Midwifery training is for those who feel a calling to work with the dying, whether in a professional capacity (hospice, palliative care, private practice) or to support loved ones. It is also deeply healing for those who have experienced loss and wish to understand the dying process from a spiritual perspective.

Prerequisites

There are no formal prerequisites for Soul Midwifery training, though a background in healing or counselling is helpful. Above all, what is required is an open heart and a willingness to sit with the sacred mystery of death.
