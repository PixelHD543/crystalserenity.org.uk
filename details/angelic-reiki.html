What is Angelic Reiki?

Angelic Reiki is a gentle, yet powerful healing modality that works with the high-frequency energies of the Angelic Kingdom. Unlike traditional Reiki, Angelic Reiki is a vibrational healing practice that connects you directly with angelic beings who work alongside the practitioner to facilitate deep healing on all levels — physical, emotional, mental, and spiritual.

Benefits of Angelic Reiki

  • Reduces stress, anxiety, and feelings of overwhelm
  • Promotes deep relaxation and inner peace
  • Supports the body's natural healing abilities
  • Clears energetic blockages and stagnant energy
  • Enhances spiritual connection and intuition
  • Helps release past trauma and emotional wounds
  • Brings clarity and a sense of purpose

What to Expect During a Session

Your Angelic Reiki session will take place in our calm, purpose-built therapy room in Shelf, Halifax. You will remain fully clothed and lie on a comfortable treatment table. Cathy will gently place her hands on or just above your body, allowing angelic energy to flow where it is most needed.

Many clients experience sensations of warmth, gentle tingling, or deep relaxation. Some may see colours or receive intuitive messages. After the session, you will feel grounded, peaceful, and refreshed.

Who is Angelic Reiki For?

Angelic Reiki is suitable for everyone, regardless of age or health condition. It is particularly beneficial for those experiencing:

  • Chronic stress or burnout
  • Emotional pain or grief
  • Physical illness or recovery from surgery
  • Feelings of being stuck or lacking direction
  • A desire to deepen their spiritual practice
