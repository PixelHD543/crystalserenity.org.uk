Munay-Ki

The Nine Rites of Initiation

What is Munay-Ki?

Munay-Ki is a powerful system of energetic rites and initiations originating from the Q'ero shamans of the Andes Mountains in Peru. "Munay" means "unconditional love" in the Quechua language, and "Ki" means "energy" or "life force". Together, Munay-Ki means "the energy of unconditional love".

These nine rites are designed to heal and transform the human energy field, preparing you to become what the shamans call "homo luminous" — a transcended light being.

Cathy is a Certified Munay-Ki Mentor, following in the tradition and accordance with the Four Winds Society.

The Nine Rites Explained

The Munay-Ki rites work on your luminous energy field (the aura), clearing, healing, and upgrading your energy centres (chakras). The nine rites are:

  • Rite 1: Healer's Rite - Activates your inner healer
  • Rite 2: Bands of Power - Five bands of protection and empowerment
  • Rite 3: Harmony Rite - Heals wounds and restores balance
  • Rite 4: Seer's Rite - Opens your inner vision and intuition
  • Rite 5: Daykeeper's Rite - Connects you with the wisdom of the shamans
  • Rite 6: Wisdomkeeper's Rite - Anchors higher wisdom into your energy field
  • Rite 7: Earthkeeper's Rite - Connects you with the sacred Earth energies
  • Rite 8: Star Keeper's Rite - Connects you with the cosmic energies of the stars
  • Rite 9: Creator Rite - The highest initiation, awakening your divine nature

How the Rites Are Given

The Munay-Ki rites are received through a sacred transmission (similar to an attunement) from a certified mentor. Cathy will guide you through each rite in a supportive, sacred space. No prior experience is needed.

The rites can be given individually or as a full set. Most people choose to receive the first three rites (Healer, Bands of Power, Harmony) as a foundation, then progress to the higher rites over time.

Benefits of Receiving Munay-Ki

  • Deep healing of the luminous energy field
  • Clearing of ancestral and past life patterns
  • Increased intuition and spiritual awareness
  • A sense of protection and grounding
  • Connection with the wisdom of the Andes shamans
  • Preparation for ascension and higher consciousness
  • A greater capacity for unconditional love
