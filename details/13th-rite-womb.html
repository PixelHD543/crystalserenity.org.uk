13th Rite of the Womb

Healing rite for womb-related trauma and empowerment

What is the 13th Rite of the Womb?

The 13th Rite of the Womb is a powerful and sacred healing rite that originated from the Munay-Ki tradition and the medicine women of the Andes. It is a gentle yet profound transmission of energy that heals womb-related trauma, restores feminine power, and releases ancestral and karmic patterns held in the womb space.

This rite is for anyone who has a womb — regardless of gender identity, age, or whether they have children. It is also deeply healing for those who have experienced miscarriage, abortion, stillbirth, hysterectomy, or any form of womb trauma.

What the Rite Does

  • Heals wounds held in the womb space
  • Releases ancestral patterns related to the feminine lineage
  • Clears grief, shame, or guilt associated with the womb
  • Restores the natural flow of creative and life force energy
  • Empowers you to reclaim your feminine power
  • Brings healing to your bloodline (past, present, and future generations)
  • Supports fertility and reproductive health

How the Rite Is Given

The 13th Rite of the Womb is a gentle, non-invasive transmission. You remain fully clothed and lie comfortably on a treatment table or sit in a chair. Cathy will work with your energy field, offering the transmission with your full consent.

The rite takes approximately 30-45 minutes. Many people feel a gentle warmth, release of emotion, or deep sense of peace during the transmission.

Who Is This For?

The 13th Rite of the Womb is for anyone who:

  • Has experienced miscarriage, abortion, or stillbirth
  • Has had a hysterectomy or womb-related surgery
  • Struggles with fertility or menstrual issues
  • Holds grief or trauma related to their womb
  • Feels disconnected from their feminine essence
  • Wishes to heal ancestral patterns for future generations
  • Simply feels called to receive this healing
