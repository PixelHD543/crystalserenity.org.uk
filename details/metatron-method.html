Metatron Method Training

Work with Archangel Metatron's sacred geometry and energy

What is the Metatron Method?

The Metatron Method is a powerful healing modality that works with Archangel Metatron — the angel who oversees sacred geometry, the Akashic Records, and the ascension process. This method uses high-frequency angelic energy, sacred geometric patterns, and the Merkaba (light body) to facilitate deep healing and spiritual transformation.

What You Will Learn

  • How to connect with Archangel Metatron and his energies
  • Understanding sacred geometry and its healing applications
  • Working with Metatron's Cube for protection and alignment
  • Activating your Merkaba (light body)
  • Clearing and balancing the chakras with Metatronic energy
  • Accessing the Akashic Records for healing and guidance
  • Distant healing using the Metatron Method
  • How to give professional Metatron Method treatments

Course Format

This training is delivered over 2 days (usually a weekend). It includes theory, practical healing sessions, meditations, and sacred attunements to the Metatronic frequencies.

Upon completion, you will receive a certificate as a qualified Metatron Method Practitioner.

Who Is This Training For?

The Metatron Method is suitable for anyone with an existing healing practice who wishes to add a powerful new modality to their toolkit, as well as those who feel drawn to Archangel Metatron's energy. Prior experience with energy healing (Reiki, Angelic Reiki, Crystal Healing) is beneficial.

Benefits of the Metatron Method

  • Deep spiritual healing and ascension support
  • Clearing of karmic patterns and past life blockages
  • Activation of your light body (Merkaba)
  • Enhanced connection with Archangel Metatron and sacred geometry
  • Powerful protection and energetic clearing
  • Ability to access the Akashic Records for yourself and others
