Past Life Regression Therapy

Removing shackles, cords & attachments to past lives

What is Past Life Regression?

Past Life Regression is a gentle, hypnotherapy-based technique that allows you to access memories and experiences from previous lifetimes. These past life memories can hold the key to understanding recurring challenges, fears, and patterns in your current life.

Are you haunted by repeat negative incidents in your life? Have you ever wondered if they originate from a past life? Past Life Regression can help you discover what past lives you have had and what requires releasing to help you move forward with ease in this life.

How It Works

Cathy uses gentle hypnotherapy techniques to guide you into a relaxed, meditative state (similar to the feeling just before falling asleep). In this state, your conscious mind steps aside, allowing access to deeper levels of awareness where past life memories are stored.

You remain aware and in control throughout the session. Many people describe the experience as watching a movie of their past life — vivid, emotional, and deeply insightful.

What Past Life Regression Can Help With

  • Unexplained fears or phobias (e.g., fear of water, heights, enclosed spaces)
  • Recurring nightmares or dreams
  • Physical pains or illnesses with no medical explanation
  • Relationship patterns that repeat (e.g., attracting the same type of partner)
  • Feeling stuck or blocked in life
  • A deep sense of knowing a time period or culture you have never visited
  • Desire for spiritual growth and understanding

What to Expect During a Session

Your Past Life Regression session will last approximately 90 minutes. Cathy will begin with a short consultation to understand what you would like to explore. She will then guide you into a relaxed hypnotic state and ask a series of gentle questions to help you access past life memories.

After the regression, there will be time to discuss your experience, integrate the insights, and understand how they relate to your current life. Cathy also offers Atlantean Soul Retrieval for deep soul healing following the regression if needed.

Is Past Life Regression Safe?

Yes, Past Life Regression is a gentle, safe, and non-invasive therapy when conducted by a qualified practitioner. Cathy is a certified Past Life Regression Practitioner and member of the National Council of Psychotherapists (NCP).

You remain in control at all times and will remember everything from your session. The technique is not suitable for those with certain mental health conditions — please discuss any concerns with Cathy before booking.
