Crystal Healing

Bespoke treatments using crystals to restore balance

What is Crystal Healing?

Crystal healing is a gentle, non-invasive therapy that uses the natural vibrations of crystals and gemstones to promote physical, emotional, and spiritual well-being. Each crystal carries its own unique frequency, and when placed on or around the body, they work to realign and balance your energy centres (chakras).

Cathy has been working with crystals for over 18 years and creates bespoke treatments tailored to your individual needs.

Benefits of Crystal Healing

  • Reduces stress and promotes deep relaxation
  • Balances and aligns the chakras
  • Clears energetic blockages
  • Supports emotional healing and release
  • Enhances mental clarity and focus
  • Boosts the body's natural healing abilities
  • Provides a sense of grounding and peace

What to Expect During a Session

Your crystal healing session takes place in our calm, purpose-built therapy room in Shelf, Halifax. You will lie fully clothed on a comfortable treatment table. Cathy will select crystals intuitively for your needs and place them on and around your body, particularly on chakra points.

Most clients experience deep relaxation, often drifting into a meditative state. Some feel warmth, gentle tingling, or see colours. After the session, you will feel balanced, grounded, and refreshed.

Which Crystals Might Be Used?

Cathy works with a wide variety of crystals, including:

  • Amethyst: For calm, intuition, and spiritual connection
  • Rose Quartz: For love, compassion, and emotional healing
  • Clear Quartz: For amplification and clarity
  • Citrine: For joy, abundance, and confidence
  • Lapis Lazuli: For truth and communication
  • Black Tourmaline: For protection and grounding

Who is Crystal Healing For?

Crystal healing is suitable for everyone. It is particularly beneficial for those experiencing:

  • Stress, anxiety, or overwhelm
  • Emotional pain or grief
  • Fatigue or low energy
  • Feeling disconnected or unbalanced
  • Physical tension or discomfort
