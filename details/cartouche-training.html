Cartouche Training

With Di Wall — Ancient Egyptian healing and wisdom

About Cartouche Training

Cartouche is known as an "Oracle of Ancient Egyptian Magic". This training, offered by Di Wall, introduces you to the powerful healing and divination system of the Cartouche. Drawing on the wisdom of Ancient Egypt, you will learn to work with the sacred symbols, deities, and energies of this ancient tradition.

Cartouche can be used for healing, guidance, and spiritual development. It is a profound tool for self-discovery and for supporting others on their journey.

What You Will Learn

  • The history and origins of the Cartouche
  • The meaning of the sacred symbols used in Cartouche healing
  • How to perform a Cartouche reading for yourself and others
  • Using the Cartouche for energy healing and clearing
  • Connecting with Ancient Egyptian deities and energies
  • How to integrate Cartouche into your existing healing practice

Course Format

This training is delivered by Di Wall, an experienced teacher of Cartouche and Egyptian Astrology. The course is typically offered over one weekend (Saturday and Sunday) and includes theory, practical exercises, and guided meditations.

Upon completion, you will receive a certificate of attendance.

Who Is This Training For?

This training is suitable for anyone interested in Ancient Egyptian spirituality, healing, or divination. No prior experience is necessary — just an open heart and a willingness to learn.

Benefits of Learning Cartouche

  • Deepen your connection with Ancient Egyptian wisdom
  • Add a unique healing and divination tool to your practice
  • Gain insights and guidance for your own life journey
  • Learn to help others with Cartouche readings and healing
  • Connect with a supportive community of like-minded students
