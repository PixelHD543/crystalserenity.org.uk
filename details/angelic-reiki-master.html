Angelic Reiki Master Teacher

Levels 3 and 4 Certification

About This Course

Angelic Reiki Master Teacher Training (Levels 3 & 4) is the advanced level of Angelic Reiki, designed for those who have already completed Practitioner Levels 1 & 2 and wish to deepen their practice and teach others.

This course is a profound spiritual journey that empowers you to attune others to Angelic Reiki and become a certified teacher of this beautiful healing modality.

Prerequisites

You must have completed Angelic Reiki Practitioner Levels 1 & 2 (with any recognised teacher) before enrolling in this course.

What You Will Learn

  • Advanced attunements to the Master Teacher frequencies (Levels 3 & 4)
  • How to attune others to Angelic Reiki (all levels)
  • How to teach Angelic Reiki workshops
  • Deeper connection with the Angelic Kingdom and Ascended Masters
  • Advanced healing techniques and symbols
  • How to run a successful healing practice
  • Ethics, insurance, and professional standards for teachers

Course Format

The course is taught over two days (usually a weekend). It includes advanced theory, practical teaching sessions, meditations, and the sacred Master attunement process.

Upon completion, you will receive a certificate as a qualified Angelic Reiki Master Teacher and be eligible to register as a teacher with the Angelic Reiki Association.
