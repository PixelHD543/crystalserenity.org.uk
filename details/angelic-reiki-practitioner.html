Angelic Reiki Practitioner

Levels 1 and 2 Certification

About This Course

Angelic Reiki Practitioner Training (Levels 1 & 2) is a powerful and transformative course that certifies you to practise Angelic Reiki professionally. This training connects you directly with the Angelic Kingdom, allowing you to channel high-frequency healing energy for yourself and others.

No prior experience with Reiki is required. The course is open to all who feel called to work with angelic energies.

What You Will Learn

  • The history and origins of Angelic Reiki
  • How to connect with Angelic frequencies and the Angelic Kingdom
  • Attunements to the Angelic Reiki energies (Levels 1 & 2)
  • How to give a full Angelic Reiki treatment to others
  • Self-treatment techniques for your own healing
  • Distant healing methods
  • Working with the Angelic Reiki symbols
  • Ethics and best practice for professional practitioners

Course Format

The course is taught over two days (usually a weekend). It includes theory, practical hands-on sessions, meditations, and the sacred attunement process.

Upon completion, you will receive a certificate as a qualified Angelic Reiki Practitioner and be eligible to join the Angelic Reiki Association.

What is an Attunement?

An attunement is a sacred energetic process that opens your energy channels to a specific healing frequency. During the attunement, the Angelic Kingdom works directly with Cathy to clear, align, and activate your energy centres, allowing you to channel Angelic Reiki energy for life.
