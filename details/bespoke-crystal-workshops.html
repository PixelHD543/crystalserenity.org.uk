Bespoke Crystal Healing Workshops

Fun, interactive workshops to learn about crystals and crystal healing

About These Workshops

Our Bespoke Crystal Healing Workshops are designed to be fun, interactive, and tailored to your specific interests. Whether you are a complete beginner or have some experience with crystals, these workshops will deepen your understanding and connection with the crystal kingdom.

Workshops can be tailored for individuals, small groups, or even larger parties (e.g., hen parties, spiritual gatherings, team building).

Workshop Options

Choose from the following themes, or let Cathy create a bespoke workshop just for you:

  • Crystals for Beginners: An introduction to crystals, how to choose them, cleanse them, and use them in daily life
  • Crystals for the Chakras: Learn which crystals correspond to each chakra and how to use them for balancing
  • Crystals for Healing: Practical techniques for using crystals for self-healing and healing others
  • Crystals for Manifestation: How to work with crystals to manifest your intentions
  • Crystals and the Moon: Working with crystals through the lunar cycle
  • Advanced Crystal Work: For those who already have basic knowledge and want to go deeper

Workshop Format

Workshops typically last 2-3 hours and include:

  • A short talk or presentation on the chosen topic
  • Hands-on activities (e.g., choosing crystals, creating layouts, meditations)
  • Time for questions and discussion
  • Tea, coffee, and refreshments

All crystals and materials are provided, though you are welcome to bring your own.

Group Workshops

Gather your friends for a unique and meaningful experience. Group workshops are perfect for:

  • Birthday parties
  • Hen parties
  • Spiritual gatherings
  • Team building events
  • Wellness retreats
