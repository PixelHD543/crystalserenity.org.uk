What is Cartouche Healing?

Cartouche is known as an "Oracle of Ancient Egyptian Magic". It is a powerful healing modality that draws upon the wisdom, symbols, and energies of Ancient Egypt. Working with the Cartouche connects you to the divine energies of Egyptian deities, sacred symbols, and the mystical traditions of the Nile.

This ancient healing practice helps to clear blockages, restore balance, and awaken your spiritual potential.

How Does Cartouche Healing Work?

During a Cartouche healing session, Cathy works with specific Egyptian symbols and energies that are drawn for your unique situation. These symbols act as keys, unlocking hidden patterns, releasing past life imprints, and bringing through healing on a soul level.

The Cartouche can also be used for guidance, helping you understand your life path, relationships, and spiritual purpose.

Benefits of Cartouche Healing

  • Clears energetic blockages and attachments
  • Connects you with Ancient Egyptian wisdom
  • Provides deep soul-level healing
  • Offers guidance for life decisions

What to Expect During a Session

A Cartouche session can be experienced as a healing treatment or as a reading for guidance. Cathy will work with the Cartouche symbols intuitively, either through hands-on energy work or through symbolic interpretation.

Sessions take place in our calm therapy room in Shelf, Halifax, or can be done via Zoom. Many clients find the experience deeply moving and insightful.

Who Can Benefit from Cartouche Healing?

Cartouche Healing is suitable for anyone who feels drawn to Ancient Egyptian spirituality or who is seeking:

  • Clarity on their life purpose
  • Healing from past life trauma
  • Deeper spiritual connection
  • Guidance through a challenging period
  • Release from repeating negative patterns
